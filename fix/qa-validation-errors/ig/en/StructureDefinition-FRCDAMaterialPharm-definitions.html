30. FRCDAMaterialPharm.sdtcExpirationTime
Definition

The date and time the manufacturer no longer ensures the safety, quality, and/or proper functioning of the material.

Control0..0
Typehttp://hl7.org/cda/stds/core/StructureDefinition/IVL-TS
XML FormatIn the XML format, this property has the namespace urn:hl7-org:sdtc.In the XML format, this property has the actual nameexpirationTime.
Requirements

There is a need in many situations that the materials used are of a specific quality or potency or functional status. The ending date for this guarantee is specified by the manufacturer. After that date, while the material may still provide the same characteristics, the manufacturer no longer takes responsibility that the product will perform as specified and denies responsibility for failure of the material after that date.
