Logical Model: CDA - assignedPerson

Official URL: https://interop.esante.gouv.fr/ig/cda/document-core/StructureDefinition/fr-cda-assigned-person Version: 0.1.0
Draft as of 2026-07-29 Computable Name: FRCDAAssignedPerson

L'élément de l'en-tête du CDA assignedPerson permet de décrire une personne physique.

Usages:

  • Use this Logical Model Profile: CDA - assignedAuthor, CDA - assignedEntity, CDA - associatedEntity, CDA - intendedRecipient and CDA - relatedEntity
